# CHM-602 Parallel handling

Lakeshore Mutual Claims Handling Manual, chapter CHM-600 Injury escalation. Synthetic training content (fictional carrier).

Vehicle damage handling continues in parallel: the primary disposition may remain the repair disposition (e.g.
APPROVE_REPAIR_WITH_ADJUSTMENTS) with ESCALATE_INJURY as an additional disposition, as long as the injury next action
is listed with HIGH priority.
