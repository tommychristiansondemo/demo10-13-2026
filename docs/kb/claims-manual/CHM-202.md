# CHM-202 Coverage does not exist vs. coverage disputed

Lakeshore Mutual Claims Handling Manual, chapter CHM-200 Coverage verification. Synthetic training content (fictional carrier).

- Coverage does not exist (e.g. the policy carries liability only and the loss is to the insured's own vehicle under
  collision): disposition DENY_FIRST_PARTY. Send the denial letter through the adjuster after supervisor approval and
  note any liability (third-party) exposure separately.
- Coverage is disputed or needs interpretation (e.g. the driver at the time of loss is an excluded or unlisted
  household member, misrepresentation questions, policy lapse disputes): disposition COVERAGE_REVIEW and route to
  Coverage Counsel. Do not deny on the first contact; send a reservation-of-rights letter.
