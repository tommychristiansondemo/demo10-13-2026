# CHM-302 Adjusting an estimate

Lakeshore Mutual Claims Handling Manual, chapter CHM-300 Estimate review and repair standards. Synthetic training content (fictional carrier).

- Unsupported lines (operations not consistent with the assessed damage): ask the repair facility to remove them or
  provide photos; do not pay them until supported.
- Missing required operations (e.g. ADAS calibration, pre/post-repair scans, corrosion protection): add them to the
  supplement so the repair is safe and complete.
- Disposition APPROVE_REPAIR_WITH_ADJUSTMENTS when there are unsupported lines or missing operations;
  APPROVE_REPAIR when the estimate fully reconciles.
