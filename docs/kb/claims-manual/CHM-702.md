# CHM-702 Deductible waiver endorsement

Lakeshore Mutual Claims Handling Manual, chapter CHM-700 Subrogation and recovery. Synthetic training content (fictional carrier).

If the policy carries the deductible-waiver-on-subrogation endorsement (LMI-END-44), tell the insured that their
deductible will be refunded or waived once recovery is made, and mention the endorsement in the plan.
