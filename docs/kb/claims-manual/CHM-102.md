# CHM-102 Payment authority (physical damage)

Lakeshore Mutual Claims Handling Manual, chapter CHM-100 Authority levels and supervisor approval. Synthetic training content (fictional carrier).

- Claims adjuster: may authorize repair payments up to $10,000 per claim after supervisor approval of the plan.
- Claims supervisor: up to $35,000 per claim.
- Above $35,000, or any total-loss settlement: Total Loss Unit manager sign-off is also required.
- Fast-track queue: glass and simple claims up to $2,500 may be paid by the Fast Track Queue without a field inspection.
