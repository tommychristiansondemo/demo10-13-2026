# CHM-303 Safety-critical operations

Lakeshore Mutual Claims Handling Manual, chapter CHM-300 Estimate review and repair standards. Synthetic training content (fictional carrier).

Pre-repair and post-repair diagnostic scans are required on vehicles model year 2018 or newer with airbag deployment or
structural damage. ADAS calibration is required whenever a sensor-bearing part (bumper cover with radar, windshield
with camera, mirror with blind-spot sensor) is replaced or removed. Missing calibration is always a HIGH priority action
for the REPAIR_FACILITY.
