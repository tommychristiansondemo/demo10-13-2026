# CHM-103 What a supervisor needs in an AI plan

Lakeshore Mutual Claims Handling Manual, chapter CHM-100 Authority levels and supervisor approval. Synthetic training content (fictional carrier).

A plan is approvable when it states the coverage position with the coverage_check verdict, the damage position, the
estimate review (unsupported and missing amounts and the net adjustment), next actions each with an owner and priority,
risk flags, confidence and open questions, and every statement cites evidence. Plans that cite no evidence for coverage
or damage must be returned.
