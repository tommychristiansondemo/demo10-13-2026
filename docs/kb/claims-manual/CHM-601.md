# CHM-601 Any reported injury

Lakeshore Mutual Claims Handling Manual, chapter CHM-600 Injury escalation. Synthetic training content (fictional carrier).

When any injury is reported (insured, passenger or third party), add disposition ESCALATE_INJURY and assign an
INJURY_ADJUSTER within 1 business day, priority HIGH. Bodily injury and medical payments are handled only by the
injury adjuster.
