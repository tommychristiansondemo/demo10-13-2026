# CHM-501 Referral indicators

Lakeshore Mutual Claims Handling Manual, chapter CHM-500 Special Investigations Unit (SIU) referrals. Synthetic training content (fictional carrier).

Refer to SIU (disposition SIU_REFERRAL, owner SIU, priority HIGH) when two or more indicators combine, for example:
- loss within 60 days of policy inception or of a coverage increase
- a prior loss on the same VIN, especially to the same area of the vehicle
- damage inconsistent with the reported cause (e.g. damage height or pattern that does not match a parked-car hit)
- late police report, report by phone only, or no independent witnesses for a hit-and-run
- pressure for immediate payment, or a non-network repair facility insisted on by the claimant
A single indicator alone is noted as a MEDIUM risk flag, not a referral.
