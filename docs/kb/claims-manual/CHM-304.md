# CHM-304 Parts

Lakeshore Mutual Claims Handling Manual, chapter CHM-300 Estimate review and repair standards. Synthetic training content (fictional carrier).

OEM parts are required where the policy carries the OEM parts endorsement (LMI-END-61) for the vehicle's age. Otherwise
quality aftermarket or recycled parts may be used if available within 5 business days; backordered OEM parts that
extend cycle time by more than 7 days justify an alternate-parts discussion with the customer.
