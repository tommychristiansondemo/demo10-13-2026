# CHM-701 When to open subrogation

Lakeshore Mutual Claims Handling Manual, chapter CHM-700 Subrogation and recovery. Synthetic training content (fictional carrier).

Whenever another party is at fault (police citation, witness statement, or liability accepted by the other carrier),
add a next action with owner SUBROGATION: notify the at-fault party's carrier, provide the claim number, and demand
reimbursement of the amounts we pay plus the insured's deductible.
