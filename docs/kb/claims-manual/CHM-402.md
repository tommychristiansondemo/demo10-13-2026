# CHM-402 Procedure

Lakeshore Mutual Claims Handling Manual, chapter CHM-400 Total loss. Synthetic training content (fictional carrier).

1. Disposition TOTAL_LOSS_EVALUATION; owner TOTAL_LOSS_UNIT, priority HIGH.
2. Stop storage charges: move the vehicle from a commercial storage lot to a company salvage yard within 2 business
   days; each extra day of storage is a cost to the claim.
3. Obtain a market valuation (ACV) report and a salvage bid.
4. Confirm lienholder and title status before any settlement offer.
5. Hold repair authorization; do not approve a repair estimate on a vehicle under total-loss evaluation.
