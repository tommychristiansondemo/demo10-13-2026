# CHM-801 Glass fast track

Lakeshore Mutual Claims Handling Manual, chapter CHM-800 Glass claims and catastrophe (hail) handling. Synthetic training content (fictional carrier).

Windshield and glass claims that are covered, fully supported by the estimate and low severity go to FAST_TRACK_PAYMENT
through the Fast Track Queue (no field inspection, payment target 2 business days). ADAS camera recalibration after a
windshield replacement is required and must be on the estimate (see CHM-303).
