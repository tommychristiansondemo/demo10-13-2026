# CHM-204 Liability claims against our insured

Lakeshore Mutual Claims Handling Manual, chapter CHM-200 Coverage verification. Synthetic training content (fictional carrier).

When our insured is at fault and a third party files a property-damage claim, open a liability feature, confirm
property_damage_liability limits from the policy, and handle the claimant's damage separately from the insured's own
vehicle. The insured's own damage is paid only if collision coverage exists.
