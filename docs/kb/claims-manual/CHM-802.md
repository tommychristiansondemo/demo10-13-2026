# CHM-802 Catastrophe (CAT) events

Lakeshore Mutual Claims Handling Manual, chapter CHM-800 Glass claims and catastrophe (hail) handling. Synthetic training content (fictional carrier).

Claims with a catastrophe code (e.g. hail storms) are handled by the CAT team. Paintless dent repair (PDR) is preferred
over panel replacement when the damage assessment supports it. Hail claims that fully reconcile are APPROVE_REPAIR;
claims with unsupported or missing lines are APPROVE_REPAIR_WITH_ADJUSTMENTS. Hail claims are not fast-tracked unless
the total is under the fast-track limit and no panels are replaced.
